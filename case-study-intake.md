# Case-study intake — what only you can give me

The portfolio's craft and narrative are strong. What's missing is **proof**: hard
numbers, evidence the research happened, and a moment of honesty. I can't invent
these without sinking you in a real interview — so fill what you genuinely have.

For **every** case I need four things. Give me whatever is real; leave blank what
you truly can't back up (a blank is more credible than a guessed number).

```
1. IMPACT   — 2–3 metrics as  BEFORE → AFTER  + where the number came from
              (pilot? logs? n=? over how long?). Even rough/pilot is fine if labelled.
2. EVIDENCE — research sample ("8 drivers · 2 depots · 3 ride-alongs")
              + ONE real user quote (paraphrase ok) and who said it.
3. LEARNING — one honest thing that went WRONG, surprised you, or you'd
              do differently — NOT a feature you'd add. The thing you got wrong.
4. ROLE     — one line: what did YOU decide that the PM/team did not?
              (sharpens "Lead" vs your PD2 title.)
```

If a number genuinely doesn't exist, say "no metric — qualitative only" and I'll
frame the win honestly without a fake figure.
